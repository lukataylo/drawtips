After the lessons

The 30-day starter plan

Skills come from repetition, not from reading. Here's how to turn the five lessons into a month-long habit — 15 minutes a day.

Days 1–5 · The lessons

One lesson a day, including its exercise. By day five you know the core theory — that's genuinely all the theory you need for months.

Days 6–15 · Build the hand

Each day: a 5-minute warm-up in the Practice Room, then one page of the worksheets. Lines first, then ellipses, then boxes.

Days 16–30 · Draw real things

Warm up for 5 minutes, then draw the Prompt of the Day from life or memory using basic forms. Date every drawing — day 30 you will be shocked comparing it to day 16.

The only rule: a bad drawing counts. A missed day doesn't restart anything. You're collecting mileage, not masterpieces — every professional's sketchbook is full of terrible drawings.
