How to use this page

1 · Warm up (5 min)

Loose arm, ghosted lines, no judgment. The warm-up page is meant to look like scribbles — it's a stretch, not a drawing.

2 · Draw the prompt (10 min)

Construct it from basic forms first. One drawing is plenty. Sign and date the page — future you needs the evidence.

3 · Come back tomorrow

The prompt changes at midnight. The habit is the whole secret: nobody wobbles their way through 30 dated pages without improving.
