Practice worksheets

Three guided sheets that pair with the lessons. Print them (each sheet fits one page), practice on top of the guides, and re-print as often as you like. Ghost every stroke — rehearse above the paper, then commit fast.

Sheet 1 · Line Control

draw.tips · pairs with Lesson 2

Top: connect each pair of dots with one confident stroke — ghost 3 times first, draw from the shoulder. Bottom: fill each lane with lines parallel to the printed one, about 5 mm apart.

Sheet 2 · Circles & Ellipses

draw.tips · pairs with Lessons 2–3

Fill each frame with an ellipse that touches all four sides. Let your arm orbit 2–3 times above the paper before touching down, then draw around twice in one motion. Bottom row: fit as many same-size circles in the open lane as you can, each touching the last.

Sheet 3 · Draw-through Boxes

draw.tips · pairs with Lesson 3

Each printed “Y” is the front corner of a box — the three lines are its three visible edges. Complete each one into a full box, drawing through the hidden edges lightly. Vary the boxes: make some long, some flat, some deep. One finished example is shown top-left.

How often? One sheet a day alongside the 30-day plan is plenty. When the guides start feeling easy, retire them — do the same exercises on blank paper. The guides are training wheels, and outgrowing them is the goal.
