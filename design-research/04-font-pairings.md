# Google Fonts pairing research — for the Night Class (Option C) variants

Context: flat `#16202F` navy ground, single `#FFAC00` amber accent, ~68px display headline,
18px body. Round-1 stack: Bricolage Grotesque + Work Sans (verified unburned and on-trend —
Bricolage is named alongside Fraunces in the top 2026 "expressive variable" trend).

**The governing principle for dark backgrounds:** light text optically "blooms" (irradiation) —
thin strokes and hairline serifs blur or vanish. Display serifs need real stroke weight
(medium/semibold minimum); body wants 400+ with slightly opened tracking. This kills the
high-contrast didones (Playfair thin weights, Cormorant) at display size on dark and favors
low-to-moderate-contrast serifs and sturdy grotesks.

## Direction 1 — Professional & elegant

| Rank | Pairing | Personality | Dark-bg note |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ 1 | **Fraunces** (wght 550–650, opsz 144) + **Hanken Grotesk** 400/600 | Warm old-style luxury; "expensive coffee-table book" | Chunky low-contrast strokes hold shape at 68px on navy. Popular but on zero AI-slop lists — just avoid the thin-italic-hero cliché. → **built as Option C1** |
| 2 | **Newsreader** (opsz 72, wght 500–600) + **Albert Sans** | Sharp literary-journal editorial | Crisp but not hairline; never below weight 500 on dark |
| 3 | **Besley** 600/700 or **Young Serif** + **Mulish** | Heritage-with-muscle, "law-school poster" | Slab/low-contrast serifs are the safest survivors on dark |

## Direction 2 — Creative & cutting-edge

| Rank | Pairing | Personality | Dark-bg note |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ 1 | **Syne** 700/800 + **Familjen Grotesk** 400/500 + **Fragment Mono** labels | French art-center gallery poster | Syne's letters *widen* as they embolden — wide low-contrast forms ideal on navy. Mono-as-editorial-accent is the documented 2025–26 trend. → **built as Option C2** |
| 2 | **Archivo** variable (wdth 62–125): display Expanded-125 Black, body normal width + **IBM Plex Mono** | Confident international-style poster; one file = whole system | Expanded heavy grotesks are the best case on dark: huge counters, zero hairlines. → **built as Option C3** |
| 3 | **Big Shoulders** 700/800 + **Chivo** + **Space Mono** | Industrial gig-flyer, loudest | Condensed heavy gothic + one amber accent = classic protest-poster formula |

Skip-with-caution: **Unbounded** (crypto-coded — Polkadot's brand font), **Climate Crisis** /
**Bungee** (single-idea novelty faces).

## Avoid list (verified 2026)

| Font / pattern | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Inter** | Burned — named on AI-slop lists |
| **Space Grotesk** | Burned — on slop lists **and** explicitly named in Anthropic's own frontend guidance ("NEVER converge on common choices (Space Grotesk, for example)") |
| **Instrument Serif** | Burned — the oversized-italic-serif hero is "the universal AI-startup hero" |
| **Geist** | Burned — Vercel-default aesthetic |
| **Roboto, Open Sans** | Template default; reads as no decision made |
| **Poppins, Montserrat** | Geometric-sans fatigue; 2015–2020 template era |
| **Playfair Display + Montserrat** | The canonical cliché combo |
| **Cormorant / thin Playfair / DM Serif thins on dark** | Legibility, not fashion: hairlines blur on dark screens |
| **Lato, DM Sans** | Gray zone — usable but add nothing distinctive |
| **Fraunces, Syne, Space Mono** | Trendy among designers but on zero slop lists — assets when paired with a distinctive layout |
