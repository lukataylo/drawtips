The curriculum

In order, from zero. Each lesson builds on the last.

No. 0

Why learn to draw

+

Drawing is often used for therapy and relaxation. Creating something from nothing helps us feel productive. But more importantly, drawing develops our visual cortex and helps us become better designers and thinkers.

No. 1

How to hold a pencil

+

The tripod grip, the overhand grip, and when to use each.

No. 2

Basic forms

+

Spheres, cubes, and cylinders — the alphabet of everything you will ever draw.

No. 3

Complex forms

+

Combining basic forms into believable objects.
