Why learn to draw

+

Drawing is often used for therapy and relaxation. Creating something from nothing helps us feel productive. But more importantly, drawing develops our visual cortex and helps us become better designers and thinkers.

About the author

I hope this small guide helps you skip the paralysis and simply make a start.
