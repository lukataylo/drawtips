## Recurring patterns across the best examples

1. **Two-color discipline** — the strongest pages run one deep ground color + one warm accent
   reserved for every CTA. Validates slate `#596981` + amber `#FFAC00`, with amber spent
   exclusively on actions.
2. **Numbered curriculum as hero content** — lessons presented as big numbered items (00–05)
   with expand toggles; the accordion becomes the visual centerpiece, not a utility widget.
3. **One doodle per section** — illustration-forward pages don't illustrate everything; a single
   small hand-drawn motif per section on lots of off-white space.
4. **Conversational first-person copy + author-at-the-end** — educator side-projects close with
   a short personal note with a photo; it reads as the trust anchor.
5. **Craft-material metaphors** — notebook paper, chalkboards, stickers, tape, sketch marks
   instantly signal "handmade and playful."
6. **Fear-disarming headlines** — the best education pages open by naming the learner's anxiety,
   not by describing the product.
